## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**  
A: Yes. The audit is 100% free, no credit card required, no signup needed. We make money by connecting high-spend teams with Credex's cloud credit marketplace — not by charging for the audit.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**  
A: Very. The engine uses official vendor pricing data (not estimates) and only recommends changes that are realistically implementable. We don't inflate numbers — if your stack is already efficient, we'll tell you that.

**Q: Do you store my data?**  
A: We store your audit results so you can share them via a permanent link. We don't store passwords, API keys, or usage data. The only personal info we collect is your email — and only if you opt in to receive the full report.

**Q: What AI tools do you support?**  
A: Currently: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Google Gemini, and Windsurf. We're adding more every week.

**Q: Can I audit my whole company's spend?**  
A: The free tool handles individual team audits. For company-wide procurement audits across 50+ seats, book a consultation with our team — we'll run a deeper analysis with volume discount modeling.
