# USER_INTERVIEWS.md

*Recent conversations with founders and engineering leaders who are building the next generation of AI-native startups. These chats focused on how AI tools are integrated into their daily workflows and where the financial friction points are.*

---

## Interview 1: Saurav Kumar — Founder, Dotplayz (dotsplays.vercel.app)

**Background:** Building a next-gen game studio. Dotplayz is a lean, high-velocity team that relies on "Vibecoding" — a high-abstraction development style where AI handles the heavy lifting of asset creation and boilerplate.

**The Tech Stack:** 
- **Coding:** Cursor & Claude (for game logic)
- **Assets:** Gemini (concept art) → Meshy AI (converting 2D assets to 3D models)
- **Workflow:** AI is used for *every* task, from marketing copy to character rigging.

**Key Quotes:**

> "At Dotplayz, we're basically 'vibecoding' our way to a full release. We don't have a 50-person art team. We have Gemini and Meshy AI. If we aren't using an AI tool for a task, we're doing it wrong."

> "The spend is invisible until it's not. You sign up for one tool for 3D generation, another for textures, another for code... suddenly your monthly burn is 20% AI subscriptions."

> "An audit tool is only as good as the money it saves. If it's just a dashboard that shows me a pretty graph, I don't care. But if it tells me 'Hey, you're paying for Meshy AI seats you aren't using,' that's actual runway I'm getting back."

**Strategic Insight:**  
Dotplayz represents the "AI-Native" startup. Their problem isn't redundancy; it's **volume**. Because they use AI for *everything*, they have a massive surface area for small leaks to turn into large losses. The audit engine needs to focus on seat-utilization and plan-scaling for them.

## Interview 2: Senior Lead — Fluxypy (www.fluxypy.com)

**Background:** A senior-led SaaS product house. Fluxypy builds professional software services and relies on a high-end AI toolchain to maintain a competitive edge with a smaller, more elite team.

**The Tech Stack:**
- **Primary Engines:** Claude & Antigravity (for deep logic and architectural work)
- **Design/Dev:** Stitch (for rapid UI/UX to code transitions)

**Key Quotes:**

> "We don't hire 'junior devs' anymore. We hire seniors and give them Antigravity. It makes one person as productive as four, but that person now has a $100/mo 'tool tax' on their head."

> "We rely on Claude for the heavy lifting, but we're starting to see tool overlap. Sometimes a dev uses Claude for something Antigravity could have handled better and cheaper via API. We need a way to standardize that spend."

> "Stitch has revolutionized our UI flow, but as we add more clients, the seat costs are starting to creep. I need to know when it's time to move to an Enterprise plan vs. staying on Pro."

**Strategic Insight:**  
Fluxypy is a "Value-Chain" user. They aren't just using AI to save time; they're using it as their primary production line. For them, the audit isn't just about saving $20; it's about **Procurement Strategy**. They need the "Plan Efficiency" (Layer 2) and "API Economics" (Layer 5) layers to decide when to consolidate.

## Interview 3: Founder — EdTech Counseling Startup

**Background:** A focused startup that provides counseling and consultation for college admissions. They use AI as a massive top-of-funnel filter to identify and reach students who actually need their services.

**The Tech Stack:**
- **Data Mining:** Custom LLM scrapers to find students needing counseling.
- **Outreach:** AI-driven personalized consultation messaging.

**Key Quotes:**

> "Finding students who actually need help is a needle-in-a-haystack problem. AI is our magnet. But running these models 24/7 is becoming our biggest expense after payroll."

> "We're an EdTech company, not a tech shop. I don't want to spend my weekends looking at OpenAI's billing dashboard trying to figure out if we're on the right tier."

> "If your tool can tell me how to find the same students for 10% less spend, I'll use it every single month. In EdTech, margins are tight — every dollar saved in AI is a dollar we can spend on better counseling."

**Strategic Insight:**  
This founder represents the "Niche Integrator." They have one very specific, very high-volume use case (finding students). For them, the audit needs to focus on **Usage Efficiency**. They don't have 10 different tools; they have 2 tools that they use *a lot*. The engine should help them optimize their API usage patterns and credit tiers.
